# Visual DNA

## Canvas and safe area

- Default raster: 1536 × 1024 px, landscape, 3:2.
- Outer safe margin: 16–24 px.
- Border radius: 18–28 px on major cards; 10–16 px on small callouts.
- Outer stroke: 3–5 px; inner card stroke: 2–3 px.
- Use a 12-column conceptual grid with 20–28 px gutters.
- Align all neighboring card tops, bottoms, headings, baselines, and internal padding.
- Keep at least 20 px between unrelated elements and 12 px between tightly related lines.

The image should be mostly navy/cobalt and cream. Orange and gold are semantic accents, not large backgrounds.

## Typography hierarchy

For generated raster text, request these visual families; for deterministic code-native output, use locally available close equivalents:

- Display title: classical high-contrast serif resembling Cormorant Garamond Semibold or Libre Baskerville Bold.
- Badges and section headers: compact uppercase slab/serif resembling Roboto Slab or Merriweather Bold.
- Body: narrow humanist sans resembling Roboto Condensed or Source Sans 3 SemiBold.
- Grammar emphasis and example verbs: italic serif resembling Libre Baskerville Italic.

At 1536 × 1024:

| Element | Recommended size |
| --- | --- |
| Main title | 58–76 px |
| Exercise main title | 52–68 px |
| Subtitle/chart scope | 30–40 px |
| Badge | 26–34 px |
| Major card heading | 27–36 px |
| Directions | 27–34 px |
| Body/example | 22–30 px |
| Note/legend | 18–23 px |

Never use meaningful text below 18 px. For projector use, prefer 22 px or larger. Use no more than four apparent font styles on one canvas.

## Alignment and rhythm

- Center titles and major formulas; left-align directions, explanations, and exercise text.
- Use tabular alignment for form tables and repeated grammar mappings.
- Keep repeated cards equal in height when their roles are parallel.
- Use identical left indents for question numbers and subitems.
- Keep blank lines at least 150 px wide when one word is expected and 240 px when a phrase may be required.
- Avoid full justification. Keep line length roughly 42–75 characters.
- Use a visible 8 px baseline rhythm: paddings and gaps should usually be multiples of 8, with 4 px permitted for fine adjustment.

## Density

- Header: 10–15% of canvas height.
- Main concept/illustration: 22–32%.
- Teaching or exercise body: 50–65%.
- Takeaway/footer: 7–11%.
- Text should occupy roughly 45–60% of the canvas; illustrations 20–38%; whitespace and framing the rest.
- If text exceeds these limits, split the asset rather than shrinking typography.

## Illustration

- Use hand-drawn ink outlines with soft watercolor wash and editorial textbook polish.
- Characters should be expressive, age-appropriate, anatomically natural, and dressed in modern everyday clothing.
- Use illustrations to show meaning or event order; never reveal a blank’s answer.
- Match illustration lighting to warm paper cards; keep saturation controlled.
- Avoid photorealism, glossy 3D, anime exaggeration, clip-art, gradients that reduce contrast, and random decorative objects.
- Retain restrained orbital paths, stars, clock/lightbulb motifs, and small scientific-editorial diagrams as recurring identity marks.

## Semantic color

- Blue: ongoing action, connection to NOW/reference time, correct structural path.
- Orange: completed event, interruption, contrast, signal word, or past endpoint.
- Red: incorrect form or explicit warning only.
- Green: correct stative form or positive completion marker, used sparingly.
